# FireWorks handoff — Forgepoint Industrial Services

**Family:** Industrial and Commercial Services. **Primary industry:** industrial fabrication and maintenance. **Related:** commercial construction, energy service contractors, equipment maintenance, specialty field service.

## Pages and modules

Six pages: Home, Capabilities, Industries, Safety & Qualifications, Project History, Request a Proposal. Reusable service cards, industry routing, qualification document framework, case-study scaffolding, and procurement intake. Primary CTA: request a proposal preview. Form fields: name, work email, organization, role, service, industry, location, timing, budget range, bid date, scope/site constraints, optional local document selection, and explicit demo acknowledgement. Up to three local PDF/PNG/JPG selections of 5 MB each; no upload or storage.

## Integration and customization

Real intake requires the client's approved secure portal or CRM, destination departments, spam controls, privacy/retention/consent terms, and document access policy. Optional secure project-document upload and department routing from **$399 setup** each, scoped after provider selection; CRM or procurement portal integration from **$299 setup** plus vendor fees. Do not assume a provider. Client supplies verified identity, locations/hours, actual services and capacity, project permissions/photos, insurance and certification evidence with expiration dates, safety program, procurement contacts, response policy, privacy policy, and chosen integration credentials.
